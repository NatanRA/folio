# Folio 0.9.7

The player's animation runs smoothly at the screen's full rate. Streamed audiobooks show their
covers without being played first. On iPhone, choosing a cover from Files works.

- **A smoother player on iPhone.** Opening and closing the player from the mini player dropped
  frames, for two reasons.
  - **Stalls during playback.** Twice a second, the app redrew every screen and decoded every cover
    on it again. That stalled for 60 to 86 ms each time, long enough to land in almost every
    animation.
  - **A 60 Hz cap.** The app was capped at 60 Hz on 120 Hz iPhones.

  Now:
  - **120 Hz:** the app runs at the screen's full rate.
  - **Covers:** each cover is decoded once, at the size it's shown.
  - **Less redrawing:** playback no longer redraws the app as it goes.
  - **Saving position:** saved every 15 seconds, and straight away when you pause, skip or leave
    the app. An unchanged position is never re-stamped as new, so a book left paused on one device
    can't pull another device back.

  On the simulator, 20 opens and closes of the player dropped 61–81 frames before and 5–9 now.
- **A smoother player on Android.**
  - **Position saving:** playback wrote its position to storage every second on the main thread,
    blocking it for up to 25 ms at a time. It now saves on a background thread, every 10 seconds
    during playback and at once on pause, seek, a chapter change or leaving the app.
  - **Less recomposing:** player reports no longer recompose the whole app.
  - **Covers:** they are decoded once, at the size they're drawn.
  - **Refresh rate:** while the card moves, the app asks for the display's highest rate.

  On the emulator, frames that took over 8.3 ms of main-thread time fell from 70–73 to 30–34 over
  12 opens and closes.
- **Covers load in the background.** A streamed Telegram audiobook's cover used to show only after
  you pressed play.
  - **Why:** the background loader gave up on long M4B files, whose cover sits after hours of audio
    and behind a large index. For a 20-hour book it read 8 MB and stopped.
  - **Now:** both apps read just the parts of the file that hold the cover: about 55 KB in 9 reads
    for that book. The full-size embedded cover is preferred to Telegram's small thumbnail, and
    after a failed read it tries again a little later.
  - **Hand-picked covers stay:** a cover you chose is no longer replaced when the book plays.
  - **Android, BookLore:** a server error page could be saved as a cover and block the real one;
    only real images are kept now.
- **Choosing a cover from Files works on iPhone.**
  - **Pictures:** any picture iOS can open is accepted, HEIC included, and saved as a JPEG of at
    most 1600 pixels. Transparent areas become white.
  - **iCloud Drive:** pictures there are downloaded before they're read. Read directly, a picture
    that isn't downloaded yet can come back empty, the likely reason picking one changed nothing.
  - **Add e-book / Add audiobook:** a book's page had two file pickers, and SwiftUI only honours one
    per view, so on iOS 26 these buttons did nothing. They work again.
  - **Android** already did this correctly, and now has a test for it.

## Downloads and installation

- **Android:** `Folio-Android.apk`, version 0.9.7 (61), Android 8+. Signed with the Folio release
  key; install over the existing app.
- **iPhone / iPad:** `Folio-iOS.ipa`, version 0.9.7 (20), iOS 17+. Unsigned ARM64 device package;
  sign and install with SideStore.

## Verification

- **iOS:**
  - 106 unit tests pass (one skipped), including 10 new ones for the cover reader: MP4 with its
    index after the audio (never reading the audio), faststart MP4, both `meta` layouts, ID3v2.2 to
    2.4, UTF-16 descriptions, unsynchronisation, damaged input, and scaling covers down onto white.
  - The UI tests pass on iOS 26.5, including the player card caught mid-flight, the mini player,
    the page book and a new test for both file pickers. The picker test also passes on iOS 27.
    `testPopulatedReaderAndAudio` still fails at its last step, which expects an audiobook on the
    simulator's Alice fixture.
  - An independent code review found a case where an unchanged position could be saved as new;
    that is fixed, along with its smaller findings.
- **Android:**
  - 113 unit tests pass (one skipped), including 11 for the cover reader. On ffmpeg-made files its
    covers match ffmpeg's own extraction byte for byte.
  - On the emulator these pass: the player morph, progress bar, theme ink, artwork editing, position
    saving, Telegram streaming, cover fit, media polish and library tests.
  - The full instrumented suite has only failures that already failed before this release.
- **Not verified:**
  - Smoothness at 120 Hz on physical phones.
  - Picking a cover that lives only in iCloud Drive on a real iPhone.
  - Telegram covers with a signed-in account.
  - Android's refresh-rate request below Android 15, which is unit-tested only.
  - SideStore installation.
