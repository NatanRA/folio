# Folio 0.9.6

The player now answers your touch at any moment while it opens or closes, the progress bar glides
instead of stepping, and Android's player is readable in dark mode.

- **Catch the player mid-animation.** The mini player and the full player are one card that grows
  and shrinks on a single spring. Tap the card while it is shrinking and it grows straight back from
  where it is; tap close while it is opening and it shrinks straight back. There is no waiting for
  an animation to finish first. Drag the player down and it follows your finger; let go and it
  either closes into the mini player or springs back open, carrying the speed of your swipe. On
  iPhone the player is no longer a system sheet, because a sheet can't reopen until it has fully
  closed. The cover flies between the mini player and the player as the card changes size.
- **A smooth progress bar.** The bar used to move in half-second steps, and after a skip it could
  jump back briefly while the audio restarted. It now moves every frame, eases into each skip and
  waits for the audio to arrive there. A small lag behind the audio is absorbed by slowing the bar
  for a moment, so it never jumps backwards. Quick taps add up: three taps of +30 move 90 seconds.
- **Android's player is readable in dark mode.** The player's title and close arrow were drawn in
  black on a dark background. Text and icons outside a card or sheet now take the theme's text
  colour, which fixes them on every screen, not just the player.

While the card is still shrinking into the mini player, a tap anywhere on it reopens the player, so
the mini player's pause button responds once the card has settled.

## Downloads and installation

- **Android:** `Folio-Android.apk`, version 0.9.6 (60), Android 8+. Signed with the Folio release key
  like 0.9.3 to 0.9.5; install over the existing app.
- **iPhone / iPad:** `Folio-iOS.ipa`, version 0.9.6 (19), iOS 17+. Unsigned ARM64 device package;
  sign and install with SideStore.

## Verification

- iOS: 96 unit tests pass, including the progress bar's easing, pending-seek and catch-up logic. The
  UI tests pass, including new ones for tapping the player card while it animates and dragging it
  down to close, and the page book, mini player and book page tests. Two are skipped.
- A frame-by-frame measurement of the progress bar during playback and skips found no backward
  movement on either platform.
- Android: 101 unit tests pass (one skipped). On the emulator the new player animation, dark-mode
  colour and progress bar tests pass. `ThemeInkTest` checks every colour theme in light and dark
  mode and fails without the fix, with the title at 1.08:1 contrast in dark mode.
- Android emulator tests that already failed before this release still fail: AvailabilityBadges,
  BackgroundPlayback, two KoSync tests, formatButtons and two PortalUi tests. `PageFoldTest` also
  fails, on the 0.9.5 code as well, after an emulator restart.
- iOS `testPopulatedReaderAndAudio` still fails at its last step, which expects an audiobook on the
  simulator's Alice fixture.
- **Not verified:** smoothness and haptics on physical phones. The Android emulator runs at about
  30 fps for the old code too, so it can't show whether the animation is smooth at 60 or 120 fps.
  SideStore installation is also unverified.
