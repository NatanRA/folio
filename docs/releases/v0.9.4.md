# Folio 0.9.4

The player now matches the rest of the app and opens straight out of the mini player, and setting
up Telegram reads as a first-time step.

- **The player grows out of the mini player.** Tap the mini player and the full player grows out
  of it; swipe the player down and it follows your finger and shrinks back into the mini player. On
  iPhone this uses the system's own zoom transition (iOS 18 and later; iOS 17 keeps the plain
  sheet). On Android the mini player's cover morphs into the large one and back, and a short swipe
  springs back open. Opened from a book page, where there is no mini player yet, the player still
  slides up as before.
- **The player follows your theme.** It uses the app's own background in light and dark mode, with
  your accent colour on the chapter link, timeline, play button and controls, instead of a dark
  backdrop coloured from the cover.
- **Telegram setup reads as a first step.** With nothing saved yet, Server → Telegram now explains
  how to create an app ID at my.telegram.org once, links straight there on both platforms, and
  connects with "Connect Telegram".
- **No stalled Telegram connection before setup.** Playing a Telegram audiobook before Telegram was
  set up started a connection anyway, which stopped halfway with an internal error and a stray
  "Cancel sign-in". It now asks you to finish setting up Telegram under Server.

## Downloads and installation

- **Android:** `Folio-Android.apk`, version 0.9.4 (58), Android 8+. Signed with the Folio release
  key like 0.9.3; install over the existing app. If you are still on 0.9.2 or earlier, uninstall
  once first, as described in the 0.9.3 notes.
- **iPhone / iPad:** `Folio-iOS.ipa`, version 0.9.4 (17), iOS 17+. Unsigned ARM64 device package;
  sign and install with SideStore.
