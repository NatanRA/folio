# Folio 0.9.5

The page picker is now a book you flip through. The reader stays readable in every theme, and there are
two new Japanese-inspired papers.

- **Flip through the book to find your page.** The grid of numbered tiles is replaced by an open book.
  Swipe a page to turn it in 3D; it follows your finger, falls once past halfway and springs back
  otherwise. A hard flick riffles through up to 20 more pages. Drag along the page edges under the book
  to jump anywhere, and lift your finger above them to scrub slowly for a precise page. At page 1 the
  book lies closed on its cover and opens onto the inside of the cover, as a real book does. Each page
  shows its chapter and its first words from the book, and a ribbon marks where you were reading. You
  feel a tick for each page passing, a tap as a page lands and a firmer knock at a new chapter. Tap the
  page, or "Open page", to go there; tap the page number to type one instead.
- **Reader controls follow the paper.** With the app in dark mode and the paper white, the reader's
  buttons and page number were grey glass with pale teal on a white page, which was hard to read. The
  controls, and the sheets opened from the reader, now take their light or dark look from the paper.
- **Accent colours stay readable.** Every accent has a deeper shade for light mode and a paler one for
  dark mode, so accent text is at least 4.5:1 against every surface it sits on. On iPhone, teal on
  white was 2.5:1. Text on a filled accent button is white or black to match.
- **Sakura and Indigo papers, and a Sakura accent.** Sakura is pale cherry-blossom paper with
  plum-black ink and blossom-pink controls. Indigo is indigo-dyed night paper with warm off-white ink
  and gold controls, softer than pure black. Sakura is also a new accent for the whole app. Papers are
  now chosen from swatches that show each page colour with its text.
- **The iPhone book page fits the screen.** It no longer scrolls to reach the buttons at the bottom:
  the cover shrinks to the space between the navigation bar and the tab bar or mini player, and the page
  only scrolls at the largest text sizes. It also no longer lays out behind the mini player.

## Downloads and installation

- **Android:** `Folio-Android.apk`, version 0.9.5 (59), Android 8+. Signed with the Folio release key
  like 0.9.3 and 0.9.4; install over the existing app.
- **iPhone / iPad:** `Folio-iOS.ipa`, version 0.9.5 (18), iOS 17+. Unsigned ARM64 device package;
  sign and install with SideStore.

## Verification

- iOS: 92 unit tests pass (one skipped), including contrast checks for every accent and paper and the
  page book's turning, riffling, scrubbing and cover logic. UI tests pass for the book page fitting
  above the mini player, turning a page in the page book and opening it, opening the book from its
  cover and closing it again, jumping to the last page, the Appearance sheet and the mini player.
- A screenshot audit covered light and dark mode × five accents × five papers across the home screen,
  book page, player, reader, reading options and page picker.
- Android: 85 unit tests pass (one skipped). On the emulator every reader, page picker, paper and
  appearance test passes; the same seven tests that already failed on 0.9.4 still fail
  (AvailabilityBadges, BackgroundPlayback, two KoSync tests, formatButtons and two PortalUi tests).
- iOS `testPopulatedReaderAndAudio` still fails at its last step, which expects an audiobook on the
  simulator's Alice fixture; its page-jump, bookmark and search steps pass.
- Packages: the APK is 0.9.5 (59), a release build signed with the Folio release key, arm64 TDLib
  included; the IPA is 0.9.5 (18), unsigned, with TDLib. Neither contains Telegram credentials.
- **Not verified:** haptics and smoothness on physical phones, SideStore installation, and Android's
  Reduce Motion path with system animations actually switched off (tested through a parameter).
